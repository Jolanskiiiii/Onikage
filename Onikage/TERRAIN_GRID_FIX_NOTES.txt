ONIKAGE TERRAIN GRID FIX

Root cause addressed:
The generated Kagehama ground atlas contained opaque near-neutral white pixels
from the generated sprite-sheet separators. Since the renderer crops the atlas
into 32x32 terrain cells, those separator pixels were repeated as visible white
tile-boundary lines across the map.

Fix:
- Removed opaque near-neutral white separator pixels from
  onikage/art/tiles/kagehama_ground_atlas.png by setting their alpha to 0.
- Preserved colored grass, earth, and stone pixels.
- Added tests/test_terrain_atlas_clean.py to reject opaque near-white pixels
  in the packaged ground atlas.

Run on Windows:
  py -3.13 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
  py -3.13 main.py

This addresses the visible white atlas grid. It does not by itself complete the
full village art replacement or verify Pygame rendering; inspect the live game
on Windows after extracting this build.
