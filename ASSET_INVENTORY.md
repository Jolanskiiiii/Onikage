# ONIKAGE Visual Asset Audit

**Audit scope:** The complete `Onikage` repository and nearby Downloads folders as inspected on 2026-11-18. Four downloaded visual packs were inspected, their text license/readme files and PNG dimensions were checked, and representative sprite sheets were viewed. Following approval, selected original files and license notices were copied to `onikage/assets/artpacks/`; Feudal Japan props are now used by the village renderer, while the other packs remain preview-only.

## Findings

- Four source archives are available in Downloads: 0x72 Dungeon, Feudal Japan Props Vol.1, Ninja Adventure, and Pixel Crawler Exterior. Curated samples are copied under `onikage/assets/artpacks/`; Feudal props are also drawn in the starting village. The other three packs are displayed only by `tools/asset_preview.py`.
- The game artwork is currently generated by Pygame drawing code. The samurai is composed on a 16x24 surface and scaled to 32x48; it has four directions and three cached poses. Those poses also provide the currently available movement animation.
- The wraith is drawn on a 24x32 surface, scaled to 48x64, and has two cached frames. NPCs, houses, cedars, village props, terrain, menu scenery, battlefield, and UI are likewise drawn from primitives.
- The village and forest are 32-pixel-tile worlds. Village ground is hand-built in code and cached: 16 grass variants, connected irregular dirt-road tiles, and stream patterns. The live village uses a winding route grid and a collision-aligned bridge crossing; no third-party tileset is used for it.
- Audio includes selected Kenney RPG Audio OGG files and its CC0 license. The full Kenney archive is in Downloads, not in the repository.
- DotGothic16 is bundled with its SIL Open Font License 1.1 text.
- The named Japanese Village & Villagers pack was not found in the repository or inspected Downloads locations.
- A nearby `Downloads\New folder` contains a Unity game distribution and Unity asset bundles, not an identified art pack or source asset set. Its asset provenance and license for reuse are not established; it is excluded from consideration and was not unpacked or imported.
- A standalone `Downloads\onikage.jfif` is not part of the project and has no accompanying source/license information in the inspected locations; it is excluded.

## Remaining integration constraints

1. The Japanese Village & Villagers pack is still missing.
2. Feudal Japan props are integrated in the starting village only. No other pack changes the game renderer, map, player, NPC, combat, or UI.
3. Before any further asset integration, select which assets should proceed and approve their visual adaptation. Keep original downloads and bundled license/terms; do not overwrite existing game assets.
4. Ninja Adventure's walk sheet row/direction order remains unverified. The preview shows the full sheet without assigning directions. Pixel Crawler tileset grid remains unverified and is displayed whole; do not slice it for maps until source layout is confirmed.
5. Keep license/terms and attribution with selected assets. Confirm Pixel Crawler terms for any intended commercial distribution before release.

## Implemented scope

1. Feudal props are used in the starting village at native 32x32 resolution and displayed individually in the preview.
2. Ninja Samurai idle/attack animations and walk sheet are previewed; the walk sheet is not assigned direction semantics.
3. 0x72 uses named stand-alone floor/prop images in a preview-only sample room, and preserves its atlas JSON metadata.
4. Pixel Crawler idle, walk, slice, and hit animations plus uncut tile sheets are shown for style comparison.

The preview creates no changes to game rendering or gameplay.
