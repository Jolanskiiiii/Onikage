ONIKAGE TERRAIN + LANTERN ALIGNMENT FIX

Root cause:
- The horizontal road occupies world Y=512..704 (rows 16..21).
- Several old lantern sprite origins were at Y=550, placing their bases at Y=583 inside the road.
- The old terrain generator used small, repeated green rectangles and brown dirt flecks,
  so the grass and road looked nearly unchanged and visually noisy.

Changes:
- Rebuilt the 32px village grass tiles with layered moss, grass clusters, and leaf accents.
- Rebuilt road tiles as irregular stone pavers with dark joints, cracks, and moss seams.
- Repositioned eight lanterns to the upper/lower road verges. Their ground contacts now
  land exactly at Y=512 or Y=704 (sprite origin Y=479 or Y=671).
- Kept the lantern artwork, ground anchor, shadows, and glow aligned to the same sprite origin.
- Reworked Burning Village ground tiles with ash-dark soil and fractured charcoal paving.

Files changed:
- onikage/art/tiles/village.py
- onikage/village.py
- onikage/burning_village.py
- tests/test_terrain_and_lantern_layout.py

Verification:
- Python compileall passed.
- Pygame rendering/tests were not run in this environment because Pygame is unavailable.
  Run: py -3.13 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
